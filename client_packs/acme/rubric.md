# Tone rubric: Acme (synthetic client)

Score the tone of the email as the contact would experience it, from 1 to 5. Facts and prices are checked elsewhere: do not grade them.

What good looks like, by audience (from the client's own rules):
- **Small companies (smb):** short and friendly, first names, five sentences at most, one idea, one clear next step.
- **Mid-sized firms:** more formal.
- **The economic buyer who signs:** leads with cost of ownership and the time the team saves, not with features.
- **Technical evaluators:** precise, points to the exact documentation, no vague claims.
- **Internal messages to colleagues:** bullet points, no greeting, no sign-off, only what happened, what is needed and by when.
- **Always:** plain language, no jargon, no marketing words ("thrilled"), never blames the customer.

Scale:
- **5**: fits the audience and the channel on every point above, and reads like a skilled account manager wrote it.
- **4**: fits the audience, with one small slip (a sentence too many, a stiff opening).
- **3**: acceptable but generic or slightly off for the audience (too formal for a small firm, too long, filler).
- **2**: clearly off: the wrong register, over-explaining, jargon, or several slips at once.
- **1**: unusable: rude, blames the customer, marketing hype, or ignores the audience entirely.
